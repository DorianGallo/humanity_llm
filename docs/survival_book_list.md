
### Recommended Survival Library (For ZIM Conversion)

**1. General Survival, Shelters, Fire & Primitive Skills**
*   ***SAS Survival Handbook*** by Lofty Wiseman (The ultimate comprehensive guide covering nearly all your survival bases).
*   ***Bushcraft 101*** by Dave Canterbury (Excellent for making fire from scratch without tools, building basic shelters, and primitive trapping/hunting).
*   ***Shelters, Shacks, and Shanties*** by D.C. Beard (Classic, detailed blueprints and methods for building robust shelters).
*   ***98.6 Degrees: The Art of Keeping Your Ass Alive*** by Cody Lundin (Great focus on core survival physiology and fire-making).


**2. Medicine (Disaster, Herbal, Chinese)**
*   ***Where There Is No Doctor*** by David Werner (The absolute gold standard for basic, life-saving medical procedures in disaster or war zones).
*   ***The Survival Medicine Handbook*** by Joseph and Amy Alton (Highly detailed guide for long-term disaster medical scenarios).
*   ***Medical Herbalism: The Science and Practice of Herbal Medicine*** by David Hoffmann (Deep dive into plant-based antibiotics and disease treatments).
*   ***The Web That Has No Weaver*** by Ted Kaptchuk (The definitive text for understanding Chinese medicine theory).
*   ***Chinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica*** by Dan Bensky (A massive, encyclopedic reference for Chinese herbs).
**2.1. Poisons and Poisonous Plants**
* ***Toxic Plants of North America*** by George E. Burrows and Ronald J. Tyrl (A comprehensive, encyclopedic textbook detailing the exact biological mechanisms of plant poisons, which parts of the plant are toxic, and symptoms of ingestion).
* ***A Field Guide to Medicinal Plants and Herbs*** (Peterson Field Guides) by Steven Foster and James A. Duke (While focused on medicine, it is vital because it explicitly details the toxic look-alikes of safe medicinal plants).
* ***Goldfrank's Toxicologic Emergencies (Medical Textbook)*** (For the disaster medical side: this is the gold standard medical reference on how to treat human poisoning, chemical exposure, and venomous bites/stings when no hospital is available).


**3. Water Extraction, Purification & Desalination**
*   ***US Army Field Manual 21-76 (Survival)*** (Covers finding water, building solar stills, and basic purification).
*   ***Water Harvesting Earthworks*** by Brad Lancaster (In-depth guide on passively harvesting rainwater and building systems to direct water to a camp/site).
*   ***The Survival Water Purification Handbook*** by Peter K. (Covers practical purification and finding water in extreme scenarios).


**4. Energy, Physics, Wind & Solar**
*   ***Homemade Solar Electricity*** by Dan Chiras (Step-by-step for building improvised or DIY solar setups).
*   ***Wind Power Basics*** by Catherine Gregory (Clear blueprints and guides for building small-scale wind turbines).
*   ***The Boy Mechanic*** series by Norman Bolster (Classic books full of blueprints, physics experiments, and mechanical projects).
*   ***Caveman Chemistry*** by Kevin M. Dunn (Teaches how to generate basic chemicals and energy from raw materials).


**5. Farming & Food Security**
*   ***The Encyclopedia of Country Living*** by Carla Emery (The ultimate, massive reference book for farming, growing food, preserving, and homesteading).
*   ***Gaia's Garden: A Guide to Home-Scale Permaculture*** by Toby Hemenway (Teaches how to build self-sustaining, low-maintenance food ecosystems).

**5.2. Mushroom Identification (Mycology)**
*  ***Mushrooms Demystified*** by David Arora (The absolute "bible" of mushroom identification, covering thousands of species, their toxicity, and look-alikes).
*  ***All That the Rain Promises and More*** by David Arora (A smaller, pocket-sized field guide by the same author, ideal for carrying in a bug-out bag).
*  ***National Audubon Society Field Guide to North American Mushrooms*** by Gary Lincoff (Excellent visual reference with clear photos of gills, caps, and spore prints).



**6. War Strategy & Nuclear Survival**
*   ***Nuclear War Survival Skills*** by Cresson H. Kearny (The definitive guide on radiation shielding, building fallout shelters, cleaning radiation, and surviving nuclear scenarios).
*   ***On War*** by Carl von Clausewitz (Foundational war strategy).
*   ***The Art of War*** by Sun Tzu (Foundational tactical strategy).

**7. Hunting**
*   ***The Complete Guide to Hunting, Butchering, and Cooking Wild Game*** by Steven Rinella (Comprehensive guide to hunting and processing meat in the wild).


**8. Chemistry** 

*  ***The Central Science*** by Brown, LeMay, Bursten, and Murphy (The definitive standard chemistry textbook. It contains extensive, safe, educational chapters on periodic trends, alkali metal reactions, halogens, and thermodynamics).
*  ***Shriver & Atkins' Inorganic Chemistry*** by Shriver, Weller, Overton, Rourke, and Armstrong (A deep, university-level dive into the properties of all elements, including the specific reactivity of metals like gallium, aluminum, and reactive non-metals).
*  ***Materials Science and Engineering*** An Introduction by William D. Callister Jr. (Essential for understanding metallurgy, how different metals interact, phase diagrams, and phenomena like liquid metal embrittlement).
*  ***Hazardous Materials Chemistry for Emergency Responders*** by David R. Bloss (A survival must-have. It details the chemical reactions that occur when different household and industrial chemicals mix, explaining the exact physics of why certain combinations are highly flammable or reactive to water).
*  ***Chemistry of Pyrotechnics*** by J.A. Bailey and S.G. Murray (A standard educational text for chemistry students that explains the thermodynamics and chemical properties of energetic, flammable, and oxidizing compounds).
